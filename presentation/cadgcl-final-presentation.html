4/49

Similarity definition

What Similarity Means At Gühring

Full Similarity

Geometry, dimensions, and functional intent are close enough for direct reuse.

Functional Similarity

The part serves the same engineering purpose even when details differ.

Contour Similarity

Shape outline or visible form is comparable, including size-invariant matches.

Target ranking: Full Similarity -> Functional Similarity -> Contour Similarity, with size-invariant comparison where scale should not dominate relevance.

Model structure

CADGCL has three working parts

1. B-rep graph construction

Convert CAD boundary representation into graph tensors.

2. Data transformation and augmentation

Create paired graph views for contrastive training.

3. Beta Mixture Model sampling

Reduce harmful negatives during unsupervised learning.

Training pair

G_original

Encoder path

Both views pass through the same encoder

G_original
shared GNN

G_augmented -> same shared GNN
z_augmented

The shared weights force both views of the same CAD model into comparable embedding space.

26/49

Training signal

Contrastive learning makes matching views close

  • Positive pair: z_original and z_augmented from the same CAD model.
  • Negative pairs: embeddings from different CAD models in the batch.
  • The objective pulls positive pairs together and pushes selected negatives apart.
27/49

Training risk

The false negative problem

A false negative appears when two different CAD models are actually similar enough for retrieval, but the contrastive loss treats them as a pair that should be pushed apart.

For Gühring, that matters because reusable designs may differ in filenames, folders, or small geometry while still being relevant matches.

28/49

Sampling correction

Beta Mixture Model filters risky negatives

The Beta Mixture Model estimates which candidate negatives may be too similar and should be down-weighted or avoided during training.

Embed batch

Measure similarity between graph embeddings.

Fit mixture

Separate likely true negatives from suspiciously close pairs.

Sample negatives

Train with cleaner contrastive pressure.
