Company context

The CAD reuse problem

670,000+ CAD models

Gühring has a large internal design base, but finding the right prior model is still difficult.

  • Fragmented search across systems and folders
  • Inconsistent metadata limits reliable filtering
  • Local templates stay hidden in individual teams
  • Knowledge barriers slow reuse across locations
  • Similia results are not accurate enough for many practical searches

Business impact

Why retrieval quality matters

find existing design -> adapt proven component

Reuse starts from validated geometry and known manufacturing experience.

|

fail to find -> design from scratch

Teams spend engineering time recreating parts that may already exist.

  • Reduce duplicate design work
  • Shorten response time for customer-specific tools
  • Improve reuse of proven components and process knowledge

Similarity definition

What Similarity Means At Gühring

Full Similarity

Geometry, dimensions, and functional intent are close enough for direct reuse.

Functional Similarity

The part serves the same engineering purpose even when details differ.

Contour Similarity

Shape outline or visible form is comparable, including size-invariant matches.

Target ranking: Full Similarity -> Functional Similarity -> Contour Similarity, with size-invariant comparison where scale should not dominate relevance.

5/49

Discovery

What the discovery process found

  • Public CAD retrieval research is promising but rarely matches industrial data conditions directly.
  • Gühring similarity depends on engineering reuse, not only visual or geometric closeness.
  • Metadata and folder structure cannot solve the problem alone.
  • A prototype is needed to compare AI retrieval behavior against current search expectations.

12/49

Literature pattern

From Voxels To B-rep

Voxels, point clouds, multi-view, mesh

Earlier and adjacent retrieval work often represents CAD as sampled geometry or visual projections.

B-rep

The field moves toward boundary representation when topology, faces, edges, and CAD-native structure matter.

13/49

Representation choice

Why B-rep Won

Voxel / point cloud / multi-view / mesh

Useful for general shape learning, but they can discard CAD topology, exact face-edge relationships, or manufacturing-relevant structure.

B-rep

Preserves CAD-native boundary structure and aligns better with Gühring similarity expectations for engineered parts.
